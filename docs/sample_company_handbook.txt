Northwind Widgets - Employee Handbook (sample document)

Holidays
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year, but they must be used by the end of March. Holiday requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Remote work
Employees may work from home up to three days per week, with manager approval. Tuesdays are "office days" when the whole team is expected on site. The company provides a one-off home office allowance of 300 pounds for a chair, desk or monitor.

Expenses
Business travel must be booked through the travel desk. Train travel is preferred for journeys under four hours. Meal expenses while travelling are reimbursed up to 40 pounds per day. Receipts must be uploaded within 30 days, or the claim will not be paid.

Sick leave
If you are ill, tell your manager before 10am on the first day of absence. For absences longer than seven calendar days, a doctor's note is required. Employees receive full pay for the first 10 days of sick leave in any 12-month period.

IT and security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are changed every 12 months. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours. Company data must never be stored on personal USB drives.
